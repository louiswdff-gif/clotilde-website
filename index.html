About

Clotilde Bouttet

Balayage and bronde, copper and brunette, honey and bright blondes. Precision cuts, and bridal hair on request.

Trained in Paris, Clotilde has spent twelve years learning to read hair: its texture, its natural tone, the way it moves. Every appointment starts with a conversation and ends with a colour written for one person.
