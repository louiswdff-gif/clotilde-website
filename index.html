The process

Three hours,
one signature.

Every appointment follows the same quiet ritual. Nothing is rushed, nothing is generic — the formula is written for your hair, on the day.

I

Consultation

We look at your hair in natural light, talk about how you wear it day to day, and decide together how far to go.

II

Hand-painting

Lightener is painted freehand, section by section, following the way your hair falls — never a foil-by-numbers pattern.

III

Gloss & finish

A custom gloss sets the tone, then a cut or blow-dry gives the colour its movement. You leave with a shade made for you.
